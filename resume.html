About

I'm neurodivergent, and for most of my career I could force my way through technical work—coding, WordPress, Adobe—but it wasn't accessible. It took an unreasonable amount of effort. These tools were built with a narrow idea of who the user was. AI changed that.

Why Anthropic

Claude is the first technology I've used that actually changed what I'm able to do. From social media to blockchain, Silicon Valley has a long history of building new tools that claim to serve people, but in practice end up serving the companies behind them.
